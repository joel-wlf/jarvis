title: Card types
description: The 22 kinds of card Jarvis can put in front of you.
---

Every card is one decision for you: what happened, where it came from, and prepared next steps. The types are defined in `packages/contract/src/cards.ts`.

## Common parts

| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Group | `reply`, `schedule`, `todo` or `know`. One tab per group in the app. |
| Urgency | 1 can wait, 2 soon, 3 needs attention now. |
| State | `new`, `notified` or `scheduled`, the same as the watch service. |
| Source | Chat and time the card comes from. |
| Say | One sentence in Jarvis' voice, shown when the card is selected. |
| Context | Extra from other chats or memory, such as a clash with another plan. |
| Suggestions | Prepared next steps. One is primary. |

### What a tap does

A suggestion carries one **action**, run by `POST /api/v1/cards/:key/act`:

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| `send` | Sends a WhatsApp message into the card's chat (through the outbox). |
| `react` | Reacts to a message with an emoji. |
| `vote` | Votes in a poll. |
| `done` | Marks the item done. |
| `snooze` | Hides it until a time you choose. |
| `dismiss` | Hides it as unimportant, for good. |
| `remind` | Creates a reminder at a time. |
| `save_contact` | Saves details to your contacts (handled in the app). |
| `open` | Opens a link (handled in the app). |

Sending, reacting and voting share the same hourly limit and offline check as Claude's tools. Done, dismiss and snooze behave exactly as they do via Claude's `watch_feedback`.

## Reply

| Type | What it is | Offers |
|---|---|---|
| `question` | Someone asked you something and is waiting. Shows the quote and how long. | Several reply options to send, already sorted |
| `voice` | A voice message, summarised from its transcript, with duration. | Reply options, already sorted |
| `reaction` | A message that invites a reaction, like good news in a group. | Emoji to react with |
| `followup` | A promise you made ("I'll get back to you") with no later message from you in that chat. | Write now, or a holding reply |

## Dates

| Type | What it is | Offers |
|---|---|---|
| `event` | A date from a chat with place, attendees and how many said yes. | I'm going, not going, later |
| `conflict` | Two dates overlap. Shows both on a time axis and the free slot. | Propose a change |
| `birthday` | A birthday with age and gift ideas that came up in chats. | Prepare a congratulation reminder |
| `reminder` | Your own reminder for today. | Done, or snooze to a time |
| `trip` | A train journey: departure, arrival, platform, delay, change. | Coordinate with a travel companion |
| `place` | A meeting point from a chat, with detail. | Reply to organise rides or arrival |
| `routine` | A habit that repeats ("call Gran on Sunday") with weeks done and streak. | Set a reminder |

## To do

| Type | What it is | Offers |
|---|---|---|
| `task` | A task with amount, recipient and days left. | Done, remind later |
| `poll` | A poll you have not voted in. Shows votes and which option fits your calendar. | Vote |
| `shopping` | A shopping list assembled from group messages, with who takes what. | Post the list to the group |
| `parcel` | A parcel with steps from ordered to delivered and the delivery window. | Done |
| `money` | Money owed either way: from, to, amount. | Settled, or remind later |

## Good to know

| Type | What it is | Offers |
|---|---|---|
| `insight` | Context from elsewhere that helps someone now, with sources. | Share it in the chat |
| `digest` | A busy chat summarised in bullets, and what concerns you. | Mark as read |
| `person` | A person you have not been in touch with, with facts Jarvis knows. | Get in touch |
| `address` | A new address mentioned in a chat. | Save to contacts |
| `link` | A shared link with domain and reading time. | Read later |
| `photos` | Photos shared in a chat on one day, and how many include you. | Seen |

## Where the cards come from

- **Derived live from SQL** by the API: `question`, `voice`, `reaction`, `event`, `conflict`, `reminder`, `task`, `poll`, `digest`, `person`, `routine`.
- **Written by the organizer** into the `cards` table in the same call that links a thread to a topic, with no extra cost: `shopping`, `parcel`, `money`, `trip`, `place`, `address`, `birthday`, `insight`. Usually zero, at most three per call.
- **Written by rules** every 10 minutes, without AI: `followup` (open promises), `link` (shared links) and `photos` (per chat and day). Tune them with `CARDS_FOLLOWUP_MIN_HOURS`, `CARDS_FOLLOWUP_MAX_DAYS`, `CARDS_LINK_DAYS`, `CARDS_PHOTOS_MIN` and `CARDS_PHOTOS_DAYS`.

**Stale items leave by themselves:** questions and voice messages after three days, and at once when you reacted to the message or topic sorting found the conversation settled. Organizer cards without a date leave after seven days without new activity. **"Already sorted"** also leaves a note on the chat, so neither sorting nor Claude treat it as open again.

**Answers to events:** "I'm going" and "Not going" send nothing to WhatsApp. Jarvis keeps your answer as a note and shows it to Claude with the upcoming events, so Claude answers "Am I going to game night?" correctly. After "not going" there are no more reminders for that event.

Done and dismissed cards never come back, and excluded chats never produce cards. Card keys are stable (`card:<type>:<chat>:<slug>`), so a reworded model answer does not create a duplicate.
