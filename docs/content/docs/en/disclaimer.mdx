## Not affiliated

Jarvis is an independent open-source project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to WhatsApp or Meta. WhatsApp is a trademark of WhatsApp LLC.

## Unofficial connection

Jarvis connects to WhatsApp through an unofficial method (the open-source Baileys library, acting as a linked device). This is not an official WhatsApp API and may violate WhatsApp's Terms of Service. WhatsApp can restrict or ban accounts that use unofficial clients, and the connection can stop working at any time without notice. Use at your own risk; consider using a secondary number.

## AI provider

When AI features are enabled, message content (text, voice message transcripts, image descriptions and surrounding context, including messages from your contacts) is sent to the AI provider you configure, for example OpenAI, for transcription, embeddings, sorting and reply suggestions. That provider's terms and privacy policy apply. Excluded chats and redaction limit what is sent; without an API key, processing stays local with fewer features.

See [What is Jarvis](./index.mdx#privacy-model) for exactly what leaves your device.

## Third-party data

Your archive contains other people's messages. You are responsible for handling their data lawfully (for example under the GDPR) and for telling them if that is required where you live.
